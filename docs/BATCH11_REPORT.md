# Batch 11 report: iconic cars, round 2

Researched 5 October 2026. 50 cars researched: 33 added (22 normal, 11 provisional), 17 held.
Five independent checkers re-opened every power and weight source; their corrections were applied
before merging. The database is at 305 cars (67 provisional, Unlimited only); 29 cars are held.

## Added
- Ferrari (all normal): F12berlinetta, 458 Speciale, 812 Competizione, Roma, Purosangue,
  12Cilindri, California T, 288 GTO.
- Lamborghini, McLaren, Bugatti: Huracán Performante, Murciélago LP 670-4 SuperVeloce, McLaren
  750S, GT, 675LT, Bugatti Chiron Super Sport (normal); Gallardo Superleggera (provisional).
- Porsche and Mercedes: 959, 911 GT2 RS (997.2), 911 GT3 RS 4.0, 911 Dakar (normal); 911 Turbo
  (996), 911 R, 300 SL Gullwing, Mercedes-AMG ONE (provisional).
- American classics (all provisional): Shelby GT350 (1965-1966), Mustang Boss 429, Plymouth
  Hemi 'Cuda, Buick GNX.
- Others: BMW M1, BMW Z8, Nissan Skyline GT-R V-Spec (R33), Lancer Evolution VI Tommi Mäkinen
  Edition, SSC Tuatara (normal); Jaguar XJ220 (provisional: Jaguar's weight is unlabelled).

## Held (details in data/held/held-cars.json and docs/PROVISIONAL.md)
Ferrari 550 and 575M Maranello, Bugatti EB110, Lamborghini Urus Performante and Diablo VT,
Mercedes SLR McLaren and SL 65 AMG Black Series, Shelby Cobra 427, 1969 Charger R/T Hemi, 1970
Chevelle SS 454 LS6, 1964 GTO, Viper RT/10, 1967 Corvette 427/435, RX-7 Turbo II (FC),
Koenigsegg CCX and Regera, Toyota 2000GT. Most have weights that disagree by more than 2%; the
CCX, Regera, 2000GT and 550 were sold in the US but no source for their US model years was found.

## Corrections after the check
California T moved from its dry weight to Ferrari's kerb weight with lightweight options (a step
had been skipped); 458 Speciale labelled lightweight-options; 550 Maranello, Urus Performante,
Diablo VT, Corvette 427/435, CCX, Regera and 2000GT moved to held; Hemi 'Cuda extended to 1971;
GT350 made a 1965-1966 record; 959 start year 1986; 911 R power made provisional (US figure
from articles); AMG ONE weight re-sourced to Top Gear; XJ220 made provisional; several misquotes
and source labels fixed.

## Open questions (in review notes)
California T body (2+2 folding hard-top entered as Convertible); 288 GTO end year (1985, 1986 or
1987); Chiron Super Sport weight equals the base Chiron's; Gallardo Superleggera weight market
unknown; R33 end year; Tuatara model years.
