# Car and Driver panels from TrackBattles (9 October 2026)

The owner supplied about 100 Car and Driver road-test panels, copied verbatim into the TrackBattles
project (data/cd_panels.json), and ruled that they are accurate and may be used. Panels are cited
with `ownerCopy` instead of a URL (DATA_GUIDE "Sources"); their curb weights count at weight step 2.
An independent check matched every quoted panel line against the copied panel text.

## Existing records upgraded
Now Daily-eligible: Audi R8 V10 Plus (4S), Cadillac CTS-V (3rd gen), Ford Mustang GTD, Shelby GT500
(S550), Porsche Panamera Turbo (971), VW Golf GTI (Mk8). Weight updated but still provisional
(model-year question only): Corvette E-Ray, Lotus Emira V6.

## New cars (20)
BMW M2 CS, M5 CS, Z4 M40i; Porsche 718 Cayman, 718 Cayman GTS 4.0, Cayman GT4 (981), 911 Carrera,
Carrera GTS, GT3 and Turbo S (992.2), Panamera GTS and Turbo S E-Hybrid (976); Mercedes-AMG GT 63 S E
Performance, SL 63 S E Performance; Chevrolet Camaro ZL1 1LE; Ford Mustang Bullitt; Infiniti G35
Sedan; Aston Martin Vantage F1 Edition; Audi RS e-tron GT Performance; Jaguar F-Type P450
(provisional: the panel is a convertible, so the coupe weight rests on a database).
Where the maker publishes a curb weight (M2 CS, M5 CS, Z4, 718 Cayman, 911 Turbo S, both AMGs), that
figure is used (step 1) and the panel figure is recorded alongside.

## Not added

  give 360 hp for the 2024 US car; held until it is clear which car was tested.
- Already covered by existing records: Subaru WRX TR and BRZ tS (trims), Jaguar F-Type R75
  (in the 2021-2024 F-Type R), 2025 Golf GTI (same rating), 2025 Golf R (328 hp; a modest change kept
  in the existing record's notes).
- Panels marked "(C/D est)" or "Not used" in TrackBattles were not used.
