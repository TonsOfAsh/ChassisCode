# Batch 10 report: iconic cars

Researched 5 October 2026 from the owner's list of cars players will expect, plus a few in the
same spirit. 50 cars researched: 45 added (37 normal, 8 provisional), 5 held. The database is at
266 cars (52 provisional, Unlimited only); 16 cars are held. Five independent checkers re-opened
every power and weight source; no figure was invented.

Also: the Supras now show their Mk ("Toyota Supra Turbo (A80 Mk4)", "GR Supra (A90 Mk5)") and the
R8s their generation ("Type 42 / 1st gen", "4S / 2nd gen"). New data-guide rules: base sports-car
versions, "M lite" models, GTs, classics and small-maker hypercars are in scope; a plain "Weight"
in a panel that marks dry figures as dry is read as kerb.

## Added
- Classics (provisional): Jaguar E-Type 4.2 Series 1, Ferrari Testarossa, Porsche 911 Turbo
  (930, 1986-1989), Alfa Romeo 8C Competizione.
- Aston Martin: Valkyrie, Valhalla, DB11 V12, DB9 (1st gen), DBS, One-77, DB12, V8 Vantage
  (2006-2008, provisional: Sportshift transmission).
- McLaren: 650S, MP4-12C, Senna. Lamborghini: Huracán STO, Aventador SVJ, Revuelto, Temerario
  (dry weights).
- Hypercars: Hennessey Venom F5 (provisional), Koenigsegg Jesko, Agera R, Pagani Huayra, Zonda F
  (provisional end year), Rimac Nevera, Chevrolet Corvette ZR1X (C8).
- Ford Mustang EcoBoost (S550, S650), V6 (S550), GTD (provisional); Chevrolet Camaro 2.0T and V6
  (6th gen).
- Audi S4 (B9), TT (8S); BMW M5 (E60), M340i (G20), M240i (G42); Maserati GranTurismo Trofeo
  (provisional), Grecale Trofeo.
- Ferrari 360 Modena, F430, 599 GTB Fiorano; Infiniti G37 Coupe, Q50 Red Sport 400; Lotus Exige S
  (the 220 hp US car; the V6 Exige was never sold in the US).

## Held
Lamborghini Miura P400 S and the US Countach LP5000 QV (no consistent weight); Porsche 930
1976-1979 (US ratings unconfirmed); Aston Martin V8 Vantage 4.7; Ferrari F50 (weights 7% apart).

## Corrections after the check
F50 moved to held (its two weights disagree by 7%); M240i reference year moved to 2023 because
BMW now also lists a 386 hp mild-hybrid version; Mustang V6 power re-sourced to Ford's brochure
(the quoted line was the hosting site's own text); several notes that cited unlisted pages or
misquoted were corrected (V8 Vantage, Testarossa, 8C, 930, Jesko, Senna, Camaro V6, GranTurismo,
DB9); the 599's spec source retyped as Ferrari's; the ZR1X's confidence lowered because
Chevrolet's US and Canadian pages disagree.

## Open questions (in review notes)
GranTurismo Trofeo: 542 hp (550 CV converted) or Maserati North America's "550-hp"; M240i: when
the mild hybrid began; Grecale: "homologated weight"; Valkyrie and One-77 market; Koenigsegg E85
ratings used as maximum ratings.
