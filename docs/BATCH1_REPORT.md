## Decisions made in this batch

- **Model years.** US-market cars now show US model years (2006–2013 for the C6 Z06), which is how
  US enthusiasts refer to them and what manufacturers publish. Other cars show the calendar years
  they were built. Eleven of the original cars were converted where their own sources state the
  model years. Ten original US-market cars still show calendar years because their sources do not
  state model years; the validator lists them.
- **BMW M5 (E39) power.** BMW NA's 2000 sheet says 400 hp; its 2002 sheet and its own 2019 history
  say 394 hp for the same engine. The record uses 394 hp.
- **Weight with a choice of gearbox.** The manual car's weight is used where a manual was offered,
  even when the automatic was the standard gearbox (992.1 Carrera S: 3,298 lb manual, 3,382 lb PDK).
- **Displacement** is the cc figure rounded to one decimal (992.1 Turbo S: 3,745 cc is 3.7, although
  Porsche called it "3.8 liter" at launch).
- **Reference year follows the weight table.** Where GM published only an estimated weight for the
  launch year, the record's reference year is the first year with a final figure.
