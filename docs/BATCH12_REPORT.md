# Batch 12 report: last icons before launch

Researched 6 October 2026. 27 cars researched: 16 added (8 normal, 8 provisional), 10 held, 1
out of scope. Four independent checkers re-opened every power and weight source; their
corrections were applied before merging. The database is at 321 cars (74 provisional, Unlimited
only); 39 cars are held.

- Normal: Cadillac Escalade-V, Audi R8 V10 Performance and R8 V10 Performance RWD (4S), Subaru
  Impreza 22B STI, Koenigsegg One:1, McLaren Speedtail (dry weight), Porsche 911 S/T, Lamborghini
  Huracán Tecnica and Sterrato (dry weights).
- Provisional: Ford F-150 SVT Lightning (2nd gen), Dodge Ram SRT-10, Audi R8 V8 (Type 42),
  Mercedes 190 E 2.5-16 Evolution II, Nissan Silvia Spec-R (S15), Toyota Supra Turbo (A70 Mk3),
  Toyota Celica All-Trac Turbo (ST185).

## Held (details in docs/PROVISIONAL.md)

## Out of scope

## Notes
- R8 V10 Performance RWD covers 2022-2023; the 2021 "R8 V10 RWD" (532 hp) is a different name and
  rating and could be its own record later.
- Koenigsegg One:1: one car was privately imported to the US; it was not federalised or sold through
  a US dealer, so the home market is used. Its power is the kW figure converted (1,341 hp); the
  Jesko and Agera R store Koenigsegg's metric "hp" as published (review note).
